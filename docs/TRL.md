# Technology readiness (evidence-based)

**Level: TRL 4 on synthetic scenarios on a linearised network.** TRL 5 is not claimed.

## Evidence
8 unit tests pass. `research/run_study.py` (8x8 grid, 1000 test leaks per setting):

| Sensors | Top-1 | Top-3 | Mean hop error | Baseline top-1 | Conformal coverage (target 0.90) | Mean set size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 0.37 | 0.65 | 1.41 | 0.05 | 0.885 | 5.7 |
| 8 | 0.68 | 0.89 | 0.57 | 0.11 | 0.921 | 2.4 |
| 12 | 0.83 | 0.94 | 0.32 | 0.17 | 0.913 | 1.4 |

With demand noise three times larger than the model assumes, coverage fell to 0.48 / 0.52 / 0.58 (4 / 8 / 12 sensors). Re-calibrating on 100 shifted scenarios restored it (0.92 / 0.90 / 0.91) at the cost of larger sets (23.6 / 12.4 / 9.7 nodes).

## Not shown
Everything here is synthetic and generated by the same linear model the localiser uses, so accuracy is optimistic. No EPANET model, no field pressure data, no real leak.

## Next steps toward TRL 5
Public leak benchmark data (for example the Battle of the Leakage Detection and Isolation Methods networks) or a utility's logged pressure data with known leaks; nonlinear hydraulic simulation (EPANET) for scenario generation.
